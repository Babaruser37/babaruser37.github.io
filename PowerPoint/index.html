Useful Shortcuts

F5 Launch slideshow from beginning

Only When in Presentation Mode:

Shortcut Action
Esc Exit slideshow
Left Arrow Go to previous slide
Right Arrow or Space Go to next slide
B Black Screen
W White Screen

How to Queue Presentations

Let's say you have a 2 hour time slot and there are going to be 5 different presenters coming up and presenting without breaks in between. Instead of it taking time for the presenter to find their slide in the sea of opened tabs, you can queue up the slide to automatically start as the last one ends.

This is done by opening the slides and starting the presentation in reverse order.

This only works in Presenter Mode.
